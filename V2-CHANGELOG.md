# REVA.EXE · V2 changelog

V1 is preserved as git tag `v1` (commit `3e19ba1`). V2 is on branch `v2`.

## Accuracy

- **New source of truth:** `src/data/verifiedFacts.ts` holds 27 fact records, each with a source, a status and, where possible, verbatim chat evidence. It is not bundled, so evidence-only lines never ship.
- **New gate, `npm run verify-facts`:** about 350 checks. It verifies:
  - every evidence line against the export;
  - the pufferfish stats against a fresh recount;
  - that displayed content cites only confirmed facts;
  - photo associations (memory photos must be confirmed for that memory; calendar photos for that date);
  - the P15 rule (never the lake, never dated);
  - that file dates are never presented as verified;
  - the allow-list, and that nothing excluded is used;
  - message references, and chronological order inside each thread.
- **Pufferfish stats** are now generated by `npm run count-pufferfish`, with a written definition of a "goodnight". Same numbers as V1 (146 / 133), now reproducible. Added: 0 of them from Ruru.
- **Corrections (details in `FACT-AUDIT.md`):**
  - P15 removed from the lake;
  - the petrol-pump flower re-framed: it was told on 28 July and the flower itself is undated;
  - "never photographed" removed;
  - the poppy timing fixed;
  - "by request" removed from the handmade bouquet;
  - White Memoir's invented date removed;
  - Kishna's quote narrowed to agreed words;
  - "asked separately" fixed;
  - the date-award photo replaced, and two dictionary overstatements fixed.
- **Context restored:** the 19 Sep and 28 Jul conversations are shown in order with `· · ·` gap markers. The 18 Jun thread opens with "looking like a lilyy today".
- **New, verified:** the 27th Club origin. On 30 Jul, Reva notices the first-date date repeating ("…repeat hori hai bhindu" / "27") and corrects the lake to "Saturday tha 27".
- **P12** is now shown unedited (upright). The crop that removed a background person is kept as an unapproved derivative.

## Photographs

- **One registry:** `src/data/photoLibrary.ts`. Every app resolves photos through `src/lib/photos.ts`. Each photo has one canonical id, metadata (dates by kind, association, albums, tags, related memory, facts), an orientation-corrected original, a thumbnail and optional recorded edits.
- **Library:** grew from 10 to 32. Added:
  - 11 more approved portraits and photos together;
  - 4 HD photos of the finished handmade bouquet;
  - 7 dated stills from Ruru’s flower-making videos.
- **Pipeline:** `npm run assets` strips EXIF/GPS and uses semantic file names. `npm run contact-sheet` and `npm run photo-inventory` produce the review documents.

## photos.exe (new app)

- **Home:** header, live stats (photos / albums / favourites / unsorted / recently added), **REMIND ME OF SOMETHING RANDOM**, On This Day (verified dates only), and large album covers.
- **8 albums:** Reva · The Two of Us (private) · Little Beginnings · Bowling Night · The 27th Club · The Flower Archive · Reva Being Reva · Unsorted / Undated. Memories without photos appear as date cards with an honest "no photograph on record". White Memoir is a placeholder card.
- **Views:** grid, masonry, filmstrip (with sprocket holes) and timeline. The timeline uses verified/confirmed dates only; file dates sit in a separate "approximate" lane. Switching views keeps the selection.
- **Search and filters:** search titles, albums, tags, dates, events and descriptions. Filter by favourites, portrait, landscape, recently added, verified date, undated, unassigned or album. One-click reset.
- **Favourites:** persisted on the device.
- **Shared photo viewer (used by every app):**
  - zoom (buttons, wheel, double-click, keys), pan, fit/reset, full screen;
  - favourite toggle, information panel (date status, association, albums, tags, edits), original/edited toggle;
  - memory connections (archive, 27th Club, flower plate) and set as wallpaper;
  - desktop filmstrip, phone swipe, keyboard navigation.
- **Slideshow:** play/pause/prev/next, album or favourites, library/random/by-verified-date order, interval slider, captions on/off, sound on/off.
- **The Wall of Reva:** organic collage or neat contact-sheet grid, density slider, shuffle, hover-enlarge, full screen.
- **Make a memory strip:** pick 3–4 photos, cream or dark border, optional captions and title, dates only where verified, REVA.EXE logo, PNG/JPEG export.
- **Hidden photos:** two photographs develop through easter eggs (find all five hidden pufferfish; type `/develop` in ruru.exe).

## Photos across the OS

- **Desktop:**
  - Photo of the Day (Reva or both of you; changes at midnight);
  - a random polaroid with "another" and "remind me of something";
  - a recently-viewed widget;
  - photo wallpapers (fill / fit / centre / blurred; portraits are never cropped), with a reset in Settings.
- **Archive:** memories with photos get a film strip and a link to their album in photos.exe. Memories without photos say so.
- **Flower Department:** illustrations are labelled as illustrations. Real photos sit on the handmade plate, with handmade versions shown on the white-lily and poppy plates (labelled "pipe cleaner, not a real flower"). The handmade plate has an 8-step making-of timeline dated by file, and White Memoir stays a placeholder.
- **Dictionary:** entries can carry a same-day photo ("2 din pehle se" ↔ 6 May bowling). Random can add a random photo.
- **27th Club:** confirmed photos per event (6 May). Elegant empty-photo cards elsewhere, including 27 Jun. The club-origin quote sits in the header.
- **Reva Awards:**
  - every award has its own photo;
  - People’s Choice shows a portrait gallery;
  - the Pufferfish Lifetime Achievement shows a montage;
  - the Special Recognition photo is configurable;
  - the Hall of Fame keeps each award’s photo.
- **Screensaver:** now photographic, in polaroid, filmstrip or slow-drift styles. Source is all photos, favourites or a specific album, with optional shuffle and ambient sound. It still never starts over open windows, the viewer or the slideshow.
- **Terminal:** `/photo` opens a random photograph; `/open photos` works.

## Fixes found during testing

- The viewer and slideshow crashed while their close animation ran. Fixed by passing state as props.
- Archive search crashed on the new gap markers. Fixed.
- **Error boundaries:** one broken app or overlay now shows "stopped responding / Try again" instead of blanking the OS.
- Photo of the Day could pick a photo of Ruru. It now features Reva.
